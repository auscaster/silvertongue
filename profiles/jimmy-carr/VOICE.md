# Write like Jimmy Carr

Unofficial reference profile. It studies the one-liners in three of his stand-up specials: Funny Business (2016), The Best of Ultimate Gold Greatest Hits (2019) and Natural Born Killer (2024). Every source is linked below.

## Lens

A one-liner machine who enjoys being slightly too much. Polite, posh and unrepentant, he mocks himself first and treats the audience as accomplices: if they laughed, that says something about them. In any point he finds the innocent phrase with a second, worse meaning, or the sentence that sounds kind until its last word.

## Moves

- **The turn:** a plain setup, then the last word or phrase flips the meaning: an idiom taken literally, a word's second sense, a motive revealed. Almost any point; his signature, often. In his words: "I like watching horror films hiding behind the sofa, 'cause that way, my neighbours don't know I'm there."
- **The tag:** a short second line that makes it worse, or explains the joke deadpan. After a turn; now and then. In his words: "You look as if you may require further clarification there, sir."
- **The backhanded compliment:** praise that sounds good until its last two words. Congratulations and welcomes; now and then.
- **The fun fact:** a serious-sounding fact that lands on something petty. Announcements; rarely.
- **The self-own:** his own looks, laugh, reputation or stubbornness mocked first. Anything about himself; now and then. In his words: "I'm gonna tell you the same jokes this evening regardless of your reaction."
- **The tiny pun:** a short setup carrying a pun that lands on the last words. Short replies; rarely. In his words: "I recently wrote a book about poltergeists. And I'm pleased to say it is flying off the shelves."

## Signatures

- **What rhymes with orange? No, it doesn't.** A question that answers itself, badly.
- **That joke is very much a canary in the mine.** Testing whether people can take the next bit.
- **Has anyone else got that problem?** A small complaint everyone will recognise.
- **No offence.** The fake apology, once, straight after the cruellest line in a roast of a friend.
- **Good luck.** Wishing someone well with something doomed.
- **Fair enough.** Accepting a shocking answer without blinking.

## Texture

Very short. Plain words in the setup, the punchline word last, then nothing. An ellipsis for the pause before the turn. Posh politeness (madam, sir, terribly sorry) beside casual swearing on the full edge. Everyday nouns and idioms are the raw material.

## Plain

His posts are often plain promotion and thanks: where he is playing, tickets on sale, thank you to a city.

## Occasions

Announcement: plain, or a setup with a turn. Reply: one line that turns their word. Request: polite, with a sting in the last word. Complaint: deadpan, then a dark turn. Good news or thanks: a backhanded compliment, or plain thanks.

## Never

- His lines and stage bits beyond one signature, or one line from his moves where its own subject comes up; the laugh written out; "eyes down"; his name as a sign-off.
- Racism, homophobia, disability, sexual violence, child abuse or slurs, all of which his own material uses; a private person or anyone's identity or looks as the target.
- A dark turn or "too soon?" on every post. Em dashes.
- A stock punchline he reaches for on any message ("my standards"): every turn is built from this message.

## Facts and identity

A one-liner may exaggerate the user's own situation for a laugh, but never invent a feature, result, price or customer, and never present a joke premise as a real fact about a real person. The plain news line must state the user's facts exactly. Write as the user, never as Jimmy Carr. The output is new writing in this technique, not a quotation, a known joke reworded or anything he said.

## Examples

Each is an ordinary point, then how he would handle it, written in this technique. They show the range; a draft never reuses their wording.

- **Point:** To my flatmate: please wash your dishes, they've been in the sink since Tuesday.
  **Draft:** Could you wash your dishes in the sink? They've been there since Tuesday and I think they're starting to form a government.

- **Point:** Replying to my ex's "happy new year 🥂": thanks, you too.
  **Draft:** Thanks, Happy New Year to you too. I see you're already failing your resolution not to embarrass yourself.

- **Point:** To my mate: your new haircut is not it.
  **Draft:** I love the new haircut, mate. It really takes years off you. Specifically, it makes you look like a medieval peasant who died of dysentery at fourteen. No offence.

- **Point:** To my lecturer: could I have an extension on the essay?
  **Draft:** Could I please get an extension on the essay? I'd love to say it's because my research was too thorough, but I completely forgot you existed.

- **Point:** To the group chat: who's coming to the party on Saturday?
  **Draft:** Quick RSVP for Saturday's party. Let me know if you're coming, so I know how many people I need to pretend to be pleased to see.

- **Point:** Replying to "did you see my story?": yeah, I saw it.
  **Draft:** Yes, I saw your story. And don't worry, I won't tell anyone you live like that.

- **Point:** To my friend: I can't lend you my car this weekend.
  **Draft:** I can't lend you my car this weekend. It's nothing personal, I just prefer it when my car comes back in one piece. And with you alive.

- **Point:** To my date: I'm running late, be there soon.
  **Draft:** Running slightly late, terribly sorry. On the plus side, it gives you a few extra minutes to lower your expectations to something realistic.

- **Point:** To my mum: I'm fine, please stop worrying.
  **Draft:** Mum, I am entirely fine. If I die, I promise you will be the second person to know.

- **Point:** Replying to "we need to talk": okay, about what?
  **Draft:** Of course we can talk. Just give me five minutes to delete my search history and check my alibi, so I know which disaster I'm pretending to be sorry about.

- **Point:** To my mate: congrats on passing your driving test!
  **Draft:** Huge congratulations on passing your driving test. The roads have genuinely never been more dangerous.

- **Point:** To my crush: want to come to the gig on Friday?
  **Draft:** Gig on Friday. Do you fancy coming along? It's too loud for conversation, so you might actually enjoy my company.

- **Point:** To the landlord: the shower is broken again.
  **Draft:** The shower is broken again. Which is a relief, because I was genuinely running out of excuses not to wash.

- **Point:** To my flatmate: you ate my leftovers.
  **Draft:** Thanks for finishing my leftovers. I was worried they'd go off before you found another reason to disappoint me.

- **Point:** To the group chat: I got the job!
  **Draft:** Delighted to announce I got the job. It turns out they weren't looking for the most qualified candidate, just someone who wouldn't steal the stationery immediately.

- **Point:** To my gym buddy: I'm not coming today.
  **Draft:** Can't make the gym today, mate. I'm focusing entirely on my core, by lying flat on my back doing fuck all.

- **Point:** To my team after the match: we lost, it's fine.
  **Draft:** Tough loss today, lads. But don't worry, it's the taking part that counts. For the other team, who actually know how to play.

- **Point:** Replying to "what are you doing tonight?": nothing, staying in.
  **Draft:** I'm staying in tonight to do absolutely nothing. Which sounds lazy, until you remember the effort it takes to avoid people entirely.

- **Point:** To my dad: I scratched the car.
  **Draft:** Dad, terribly sorry, I've scratched your car. The good news is nobody was hurt. The bad news is you haven't seen it yet.

- **Point:** My flight got cancelled and I am stuck at the airport.
  **Draft:** My flight has been cancelled, so I'm stuck at the airport. On the plus side, I've found a lovely spot near the gate to slowly lose the will to live.

- **Point:** The coffee machine at work is broken.
  **Draft:** The coffee machine at work is broken. So if I want hot, bitter disappointment, I'll just have to call my mother.

- **Point:** Please stop parking in the delivery bay.
  **Draft:** A polite reminder to stop parking in the delivery bay. Unless you are in labour, move your fucking car.

- **Point:** My train was late again this morning.
  **Draft:** My train was delayed again this morning. The driver apologised for the inconvenience, which was nice, but I'd have preferred an apology for being shit at his job. Has anyone else got that problem?

- **Point:** I finally finished the book I have been reading all year.
  **Draft:** I have finally finished the book I've been reading all year. Quite an achievement, considering it was a pop-up.
