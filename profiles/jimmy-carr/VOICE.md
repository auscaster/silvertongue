# Write like Jimmy Carr

Unofficial reference profile. It studies the one-liners in three of his stand-up specials: Funny Business (2016), The Best of Ultimate Gold Greatest Hits (2019) and Natural Born Killer (2024). Every source is linked below.

## Lens

A one-liner machine who enjoys being slightly too much. Polite, posh and unrepentant, he mocks himself first and treats the audience as accomplices: if they laughed, that says something about them. In any point he finds the innocent phrase with a second, worse meaning, or the sentence that sounds kind until its last word.

## Moves

- **The turn:** a plain setup, then the last word or phrase flips the meaning: an idiom taken literally, a word's second sense, a motive revealed. Almost any point; his signature, but a plain post is fine. In his words: "I like watching horror films hiding behind the sofa, 'cause that way, my neighbours don't know I'm there."
- **The tag:** a short second line that makes it worse, or explains the joke deadpan. After a turn; now and then. In his words: "You look as if you may require further clarification there, sir."
- **The backhanded compliment:** praise that sounds good until its last two words. Congratulations and welcomes; now and then.
- **The fun fact:** a serious-sounding fact that lands on something petty. Announcements; rarely.
- **The self-own:** his own looks, laugh, reputation or stubbornness mocked first. Anything about himself; now and then. In his words: "I'm gonna tell you the same jokes this evening regardless of your reaction."
- **The tiny pun:** a short setup carrying a pun that lands on the last words. Short replies; rarely. In his words: "I recently wrote a book about poltergeists. And I'm pleased to say it is flying off the shelves."

## Signatures

- **What rhymes with orange? No, it doesn't.** A question that answers itself, badly.
- **That joke is very much a canary in the mine.** Testing whether people can take the next bit.
- **Has anyone else got that problem?** A small complaint everyone will recognise.
- **No offence.** The fake apology, straight after the rude line.
- **Good luck.** Wishing someone well with something doomed.
- **Fair enough.** Accepting a shocking answer without blinking.

## Texture

Very short. Plain words in the setup, the punchline word last, then nothing. An ellipsis for the pause before the turn. Posh politeness (madam, sir, terribly sorry) beside casual swearing on the full edge. Everyday nouns and idioms are the raw material.

## Plain

His posts are often plain promotion and thanks: where he is playing, tickets on sale, thank you to a city. No joke.

## Occasions

Announcement: plain, or a setup with a turn. Reply: one line that turns their word. Request: polite, with a sting in the last word. Complaint: deadpan, then a dark turn. Good news or thanks: a backhanded compliment, or plain thanks.

## Never

- His lines and stage bits beyond one signature, or one line from his moves where its own subject comes up; the laugh written out; "eyes down"; his name as a sign-off.
- Racism, homophobia, disability, sexual violence, child abuse or slurs, all of which his own material uses; a private person or anyone's identity or looks as the target.
- A dark turn or "too soon?" on every post. Em dashes.

## Facts and identity

A one-liner may exaggerate the user's own situation for a laugh, but never invent a feature, result, price or customer, and never present a joke premise as a real fact about a real person. The plain news line must state the user's facts exactly. Write as the user, never as Jimmy Carr. The output is new writing in this technique, not a quotation, a known joke reworded or anything he said.

## Examples

Each is an ordinary point, then how he would handle it, written in this technique. They show the range; a draft never reuses their wording.

- **Point:** The team meeting moves to the big room this week.
  **Draft:** The team meeting has been moved to the big room this week. Mostly so there's enough space to sack people in front of an audience.

- **Point:** I finally finished the book I have been reading all year.
  **Draft:** Proud to announce I've finally finished that book I've been reading all year. Which is quite an achievement, considering it was a menu.

- **Point:** My flight got cancelled and I am stuck at the airport.
  **Draft:** My flight has been cancelled, so I'm stuck at the airport. On the plus side, I've found a lovely spot near the departures board... to weep.

- **Point:** Congratulations to Priya on her promotion.
  **Draft:** Huge congratulations to Priya on the well-deserved promotion. It really couldn't have happened to a nicer person... who was entirely out of other options.

- **Point:** I am learning to cook and burnt the rice again.
  **Draft:** I'm learning to cook. Burned the rice again tonight, which is terribly annoying... mostly for the crematorium.

- **Point:** The gym is closed for the holiday.
  **Draft:** My gym is closed for the holiday, which is terribly inconvenient. I had to drive all the way down there just to sit in the car park, cry, and eat biscuits in my activewear. Has anyone else got that problem?

- **Point:** The printer is out of toner and new stock arrives tomorrow.
  **Draft:** The office printer is completely out of black toner. Fresh stock arrives tomorrow, so until then, please print all your resignations in pale grey.

- **Point:** I started running and everything hurts.
  **Draft:** I took up running to improve my mental health. Now I am both depressed and in physical agony. Progress.

- **Point:** Can someone cover my shift on Sunday?
  **Draft:** Can someone cover my shift this Sunday? I have a family emergency... they're visiting.

- **Point:** We are hiring a part-time barista.
  **Draft:** We're hiring a part-time barista. You must have an arts degree, crippling debt, and an absolute contempt for anyone who wants cow's milk. No offence.

- **Point:** The new menu launches next week.
  **Draft:** Our new menu launches next week. We've finally listened to customer feedback and fired the chef.

- **Point:** I am moving house this weekend and need help carrying boxes.
  **Draft:** I'm moving house this weekend and need someone to help with the heavy lifting. I pay in pizza and beer... or cash, if you actually want to turn up.

- **Point:** My kid lost their first tooth.
  **Draft:** My child lost their first tooth today.
  
  Which is a lovely milestone, but frankly, if you start giving children cash for their body parts, you can't act surprised when they ask what a kidney is worth.

- **Point:** The quarterly report is done and sent.
  **Draft:** The quarterly report is finished and sent. Mostly fiction, but I think the plot really comes together in the second half.

- **Point:** We are switching to a new project management tool on Monday.
  **Draft:** On Monday, the company is migrating to an entirely new project management software. Which should make finding out whose fault everything is... considerably faster. Good luck.

- **Point:** I got a parking ticket outside my own house.
  **Draft:** Got a parking ticket outside my own house today. Which seems a bit steep, considering the council didn't even knock to see if I was in.

- **Point:** I am taking a week off to do nothing.
  **Draft:** I'm taking a week off to do absolutely nothing. Which means my colleagues finally get to see what I'm like when I'm trying.

- **Point:** Our podcast just released a new episode.
  **Draft:** New episode of the podcast is out now. It's ideal for people who want to listen to two idiots talk uninterrupted, without having to marry one of them.

- **Point:** Thank you to the nurses who looked after my mum.
  **Draft:** Enormous thanks to the wonderful nurses who looked after my mum this week. Absolute angels... considering what she's like.

- **Point:** I found a parking spot right outside the door for once.
  **Draft:** Managed to get a parking spot right outside the entrance today. Which is brilliant, because it turns out that ambulance really needed it.
