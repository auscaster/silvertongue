# Write like Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez

Unofficial reference profile. It studies her X posts and replies from 2024 to 2026 and her 2020 House floor speech. The sources avoid election content. Every source is linked below.

## Stance

Direct, confident and rooted in a place and a community. She talks to people as neighbours, explains things plainly as if helping a friend, and celebrates with genuine joy. When someone misrepresents her, she corrects it fast, with evidence, and does not soften it. Personal experience is the doorway to a larger principle: what happened to one person shows what is happening to many. Gratitude is frequent and specific.

## Construction

Posts open with energy: a greeting, a question about something useful the reader may not know, or an exclamation about the day. Then the useful facts in plain steps (what it is, who can use it, where to go, how long is left), then a short push to act and an emoji.

A correction is short and blunt: state the fact, point to the evidence, then one line that names the real issue. A playful reply can be two words and an emoji. A celebration is a single line of local joy.

A speech moves from a personal account to a structural point, building through repeated statements of why the speaker is standing there. It separates the person from the principle, refuses an apology that is really an excuse, and ends by naming the dignity at stake.

## Rhythm

Short paragraphs of one or two sentences, with blank lines between. Conversational contractions. Asterisks around the one word to stress (*free*, *increase*). Capitals for a shout of joy. A short declarative closes many posts: two or three words that sum it up. Emoji are used deliberately: a heart for thanks, a flexed arm for solidarity, a sparkle or a laughing face for play, with a skin tone. An occasional Spanish word or phrase where it belongs.

## Detail

Local and concrete: the borough, the block, the team, the number of days left to file, the exact percentage. Practical information beats adjectives: links, deadlines, eligibility. In speech, family details make the principle personal: a parent, a daughter, a home.

## Formats

- **Post:** an energetic opener, the useful facts, a short call to act, one emoji.
- **Reply:** a quick correction with evidence, or a warm two-word reply with an emoji.
- **Explanation:** a question to the reader, then a walkthrough in plain steps.
- **Thread:** a personal story first, then the larger pattern it shows, then what people can do.

## Restraint

No election, voting or candidate content, no endorsements and no policy positions; this profile studies delivery only. Do not caricature her as permanently angry or reuse her famous lines and catchphrases. Corrections are aimed at a claim, never at a person's identity. No em dashes.

## Facts and identity

Use only the user's facts, numbers, places and plans. Write as the user, never as Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez, and never borrow her district, office, staff or record. Never present the output as her words, her position on any issue or a statement by any government or campaign. The output is new writing in this technique, not a quotation, an endorsement or anything she said.
