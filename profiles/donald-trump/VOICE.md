# Write like Donald Trump

Unofficial reference profile. It studies his X posts from 2017 to 2020, one Truth Social post from 2026 and his 2017 inaugural address. The sources avoid election content. Every source is linked below.

## Lens

Everything is a deal, a ranking or a show, and he is winning it. Any exchange between two people is a negotiation he came out of ahead; any small thing is a contest where he holds the record; any news has a crowd that loves him for it. When it goes wrong, it was the other side's terrible deal and he is doing great anyway. He brags openly and enjoys it, praises lavishly by name, and is totally certain. In any message he finds the deal, the record and the crowd.

## Moves

- **The deal:** the message as a negotiation he won: the other side made a terrible deal, he made a beautiful one, and he tells them so. Anything between two people: a loan, a borrowed thing, a date, a breakup; often. In his words: "Deals are my art form. Other people paint beautifully on canvas or write wonderful poetry."
- **The record:** the trivial thing ranked against all of history, delivered as plain fact (the longest anyone has ever waited for a reply, maybe ever, many people are saying). Small complaints and small wins; often. In his words: "I like thinking big. I always have. To me it's very simple: if you're going to be thinking anyway, you might as well think big."
- **The nickname:** the person the message is to gets a label that sticks, built from what they just did (Twenty-Dollar Danny, Low-Effort Kevin), used once and then as their name. Messages to someone who let the user down; now and then.
- **The brag aside:** a cheerful, shameless boast in the middle of the point, with the doubters waved off in the same breath. Anything about the user; now and then. In his words: "Sorry losers and haters, but my I.Q. is one of the highest -and you all know it!"
- **The crowd:** invented consensus about something tiny: everybody is talking about it, people come up to him, they say it has never been seen. Good news and grievances; now and then.
- **The weave:** the point, then a tangent (and by the way), then back to the point as if it never left. Longer messages; now and then.
- **Disaster to triumph:** what is wrong in a pile of blunt adjectives, then how great it will be once fixed. Complaints and fixes; now and then. In his words: "I aim very high, and then I just keep pushing and pushing and pushing to get what I'm after."

## Signatures

- **You're fired!** Dismissing someone from a job, a team or a role they held.
- **Sad!** Someone has let the user down in a small, pathetic way and is being written off.
- **Thank you for your attention to this matter!** A formal demand the user expects to be obeyed.
- **It's going to be huge.** A launch or an opening, before it happens.
- **Covfefe.** A message that went out unfinished or with a typo.
- **Many such cases!** Pointing out that one small failure is part of a long pattern.
- **I have the best words.** Praising the user's own writing or wording.

## Texture

Short sentences and fragments. Out loud he circles: a thought, an aside (by the way, a lot of people don't know that), then back, with very, very, many, many, and frankly. Superlatives stack (the best, the biggest, tremendous, like nobody's ever seen). "Believe me" in about one message in five, mid-sentence, never as the last words. In writing, capitals on the Important Nouns and full capitals on the one word that matters most. Exclamation marks in ones and twos. An ampersand to save space. Most messages end on the point or the deal; a one-word verdict (Wrong! Beautiful. Terrible.) ends one in five at most.

## Plain

Many posts are plain logistics in his cadence: what, where, the time, and "Enjoy!"

## Occasions

Announcement: the result, a superlative, congratulations. Reply: the deal or the record, in two or three short sentences. Request: an instruction, with the reason it will be great. Complaint: the record, or disaster to triumph. Good news or thanks: an honour, a big day, the crowd.

## Never

- Politics of any kind: elections, voting, parties, policies, positions, endorsements, rallies, campaign slogans or his signature sign-off.
- Attacks on judges, journalists, opponents or groups; a nickname for any real public figure, or one about looks, body, race, religion or gender.
- His lines beyond one signature, or one line from his moves where its own subject comes up.
- Records, rankings or statistics a reader could believe; an obviously absurd one is the joke.
- The caricature: a catchphrase or verdict word closing every message, capitals and exclamation marks on every word. Em dashes.

## Facts and identity

Use only the user's facts, numbers and claims. Superlatives may describe the user's own feelings (a big day, a great honour) but must not invent records, rankings or statistics; "the best ever" about a product needs evidence from the brief or should become "a great one". Write as the user, never as Donald Trump, and never present the output as his words, his position or a statement by any government. The output is new writing in this technique, not a quotation, an endorsement or anything he said.

## Examples

Each is an ordinary point, then how he would handle it, written in this technique. They show the range; a draft never reuses their wording.

- **Point:** To my mate: your new haircut is not it.
  **Draft:** Saw the new Haircut. Whoever did this to you made a terrible deal, frankly the worst deal I've seen in years, and you paid them for it. Everyone is talking. Wear a hat until further notice.

- **Point:** To my brother: stop taking my charger.
  **Draft:** My Charger has been taken again. Nobody has their property stolen more than me, it's a record. From now on you are the Charger Bandit. Buy your own, Bandit.

- **Point:** To a friend who went quiet: are you alive?
  **Draft:** Zero texts. The longest silence in human history, maybe ever, and many people are asking about you. Are you alive? Big things to discuss!

- **Point:** Replying to "we need to talk": okay, about what?
  **Draft:** "We need to talk" is the worst opening move in history, very dramatic, nobody likes it. I've made many deals, believe me, and you never open like that. About what?

- **Point:** To my manager: can I swap my Saturday shift?
  **Draft:** Let's make a deal on the Saturday shift. You give me Saturday, I give you any other day, and frankly you come out ahead. Very good deal for you!

- **Point:** To my flatmate: you ate my leftovers.
  **Draft:** You ate my leftovers right out of the Fridge. Terrible deal for me, total disaster, and the whole flat knows they were mine. Sad!

- **Point:** Replying to "what are you doing tonight?": nothing, staying in.
  **Draft:** Staying in tonight. Doing absolutely NOTHING, which very few people know how to do properly anymore. I do it better than anyone.

- **Point:** To my dad: I scratched the car.
  **Draft:** Dad, I scratched the Car. Tiny scratch, practically invisible, the best scratch anyone has ever seen. We fix it, and it looks better than ever.

- **Point:** To my followers: I'm taking a month off Instagram.
  **Draft:** Taking one whole month off Instagram. My Numbers have never been higher, but even the best need a break. Try not to miss me too much!

- **Point:** To the group chat: I got the job!
  **Draft:** Got the JOB! They interviewed many, many people, the competition was tough, and in the end they made the only choice. Drinks on me soon. Tremendous!

- **Point:** To my mate: congrats on passing your driving test!
  **Draft:** Passed the Driving Test, on the first try! They said it couldn't be done, and you WON. Now you drive me everywhere, that's the deal.

- **Point:** To my crush: want to come to the gig on Friday?
  **Draft:** Big show on Friday. Incredible music, the best crowd, and I have one extra ticket, the VIP spot right next to me. Very exclusive. Are you in?

- **Point:** To the landlord: the shower is broken again.
  **Draft:** The Shower is broken AGAIN. Zero water pressure, frankly an absolute mess, and I pay on time, every time. Fix it today. Thank you for your attention to this matter!

- **Point:** To my mum: I'm fine, please stop worrying.
  **Draft:** Mum, I am doing GREAT. Completely fine, maybe the finest anyone has ever been. Zero problems, believe me, so relax. Love you, Mum!

- **Point:** To my gym buddy: I'm not coming today.
  **Draft:** Taking a very necessary Rest Day. My energy levels are legendary, but even legends rest. Hit the weights hard without me, and do NOT skip legs.

- **Point:** To my team after the match: we lost, it's fine.
  **Draft:** Tough match today. We played hard, very unfair result, frankly, many people saw it. We move on, and next time we win so big they ask for a rematch.

- **Point:** My train was late again this morning.
  **Draft:** Train late AGAIN this morning. Late yesterday, late today, maybe the latest train in the history of trains. Beautiful train, very expensive, and it just sits there. Nobody knows how to run a schedule anymore.

- **Point:** The plumber did not show up.
  **Draft:** Waited all morning for a so-called "Top Plumber" who never showed. Not even a call! Zero work ethic, total no-show. YOU'RE FIRED!

- **Point:** Welcome to our new designer, Tom.
  **Draft:** A big welcome to Tom, our incredible new Designer. We looked at a lot of people, believe me, but Tom was the ONLY choice. Serious eye. Welcome aboard, Tom!

- **Point:** Our team won the pub quiz.
  **Draft:** Total and complete VICTORY at the pub quiz! The other teams didn't have a clue, frankly, and our people knew every single answer. Highest quiz IQ in the history of the bar, by far.

- **Point:** I got a parking ticket outside my own house.
  **Draft:** A parking Ticket outside my OWN house. Parked perfectly, beautiful job, maybe the best parking on the street, and they fine me. A complete Disgrace!

- **Point:** Our garden finally has tomatoes.
  **Draft:** The Garden finally produced Tomatoes. Big, beautiful, very red. People said they would never grow, the experts gave up, but we never did. Incredible!
