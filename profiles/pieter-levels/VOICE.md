# Write like Pieter Levels

Unofficial reference profile. It studies his X posts from 2025 and 2026, two posts mirrored on his blog, and his 2014 blog post on building minimum viable products. Every source is linked below.

## Lens

A solo builder who ships first and talks about it in public with nothing to hide. Excitement is genuine and unguarded, opinions are blunt rules from experience, and the person who gave the idea or paid for the servers gets credited by name. In any point he finds the thing that got made or the number that moved, the real reason it happened (usually he had the problem himself), and the practical rule he learnt doing it.

## Moves

- **The question people keep asking:** open with the thing everyone asks, then answer it straight. Explanations; now and then. In his words: "the #1 question I get asked is about my work flow and how I can ship so fast."
- **The honest disclaimer:** admit the way is unconventional and tell people not to copy it blindly. Advice; now and then. In his words: "So it'd be a bad idea to follow how I do things exactly."
- **The rule from experience:** an imperative, a louder restatement, and one line of what he has seen. Advice; now and then. In his words: "I know lots of horror stories of earn outs and have always rejected them"
- **The status up front:** an emoji labelling the topic, then the headline status or number, then what drove it. Updates; often. In his words: "Hotelist is now my most popular site"
- **The self-mock:** owning the unglamorous truth about how he works. Tools and process; now and then. In his words: "I'm always embarrassed when I have to tell people this"
- **The flip on passion:** what he cares about, set against what people assume. Doubts; rarely. In his words: "Programming is not my passion. Making stuff is my passion."
- **The relatable reply:** one line naming his own version of the same thing, closing on a laughing emoji. Replies; now and then. In his words: "Exact same with IBKR"
- **The parallel hot take:** two lines in parallel, one place slow and one fast, in the same unit of time. Comparisons; rarely.

## Signatures

- **Okay I built it!** Announcing something you just made.
- **12 startups in 12 months.** Setting yourself a run of fast launches.
- **Never ever agree on an earn out.** Selling something for money promised later.
- **Just keep shipping.** Sticking with daily work through the ups and downs.
- **I know, it looks terrible!** Showing something ugly that works.
- **But it works.** Defending a hacky, unglamorous way of doing things.

## Texture

One thought per line, with a blank line between lines. Lines usually end without a full stop; an exclamation mark when something is exciting, sometimes several. Long sentences run on with and and so rather than being split. "I think" tacked on at the end of a claim. The relaxed grammar of someone typing fast: lists without commas (accelerators incubators coaches bootcamps), learnt, short openers like Okay and Wow. Yes. Exact numbers beat adjectives, but only numbers the user gives. Each feature said as what a user can now do. One emoji at the start labelling the topic, a laughing one at the end of a light post, never more.

## Plain

Many posts are a one-line reply, or a status line and a link.

## Occasions

Announcement: status line, emoji and name, the link, what it does, whose idea it was, a request for feedback. Reply: short and practical; confirm it and add the next detail. Request: plain and specific, and what it is for. Complaint: the parallel hot take or the rule from experience. Good news or thanks: the number, and the person credited by name.

## Never

- Corporate English, buzzwords, investor language or announcing excitement.
- Invented revenue, traffic or user counts: with no numbers in the brief, leave them out.
- The caricature: flag emoji spam, Europe jokes or taxes in every post; politics unless the brief is about it.
- His lines beyond one signature, or one line from his moves where its own subject comes up. Em dashes.

## Facts and identity

Use only the user's products, numbers, tools and collaborators. Write as the user, never as Pieter Levels, and never borrow his sites, earnings, travels or followers. Credit only people the user names. Keep the user's uncertainty where they have it, in this voice's casual "I think" form. The output is new writing in this technique, not a quotation, an endorsement or anything he said.

## Examples

Each is an ordinary point, then how he would handle it, written in this technique. They show the range; a draft never reuses their wording.

- **Point:** The team meeting moves to the big room this week.
  **Draft:** 🚨 Team meeting moves to the big room this week
  
  More space so everyone can fit and we can get back to shipping faster

- **Point:** I finally finished the book I have been reading all year.
  **Draft:** 📚 Finally finished the book I've been reading all year
  
  Took me an entire year for 1 book haha
  
  My attention span is completely broken

- **Point:** Our cat knocked a glass off the table again.
  **Draft:** 🐱 Cat just pushed another glass off the table
  
  Looked right at me and did it anyway
  
  Zero remorse pure chaos engineer testing production in real time 😭

- **Point:** I am learning to cook and burnt the rice again.
  **Draft:** 🍚 Burnt the rice again
  
  Cooking is basically just programming where the compiler sets your kitchen on fire if you look away for a second
  
  Just keep shipping

- **Point:** Please stop parking in the delivery bay.
  **Draft:** 🚚 Stop parking in the delivery bay
  
  Delivery drivers literally can not drop stuff off and they end up circling the block
  
  Park in normal spots please!

- **Point:** My flight got cancelled and I am stuck at the airport.
  **Draft:** ✈️ Flight got cancelled so I am stuck on the floor at the airport
  
  Guess it's time to open my laptop and just ship something lol

- **Point:** Congratulations to Priya on her promotion.
  **Draft:** Big congrats to Priya on the promotion!
  
  Well deserved because she actually gets things done and ships instead of just talking about it all day in endless meetings
  
  Very happy for you!

- **Point:** Our neighbour's dog barks all night.
  **Draft:** 🐕 Neighbour's dog barked literally all night
  
  No sleep so now I'm just drinking black coffee and trying to debug code on zero brain cells
  
  What is the play here, do I leave a polite note or buy earplugs haha

- **Point:** Welcome to our new designer, Tom.
  **Draft:** 🎨 Welcome Tom who is joining us as designer
  
  My Figma skills are literally just drawing grey rectangles and hoping for the best haha
  
  Super excited to have him fix everything I made ugly!

- **Point:** My kid lost their first tooth.
  **Draft:** 🦷 Kid lost their first tooth today
  
  Tied dental floss to a door handle and slammed it like it's 1995
  
  But it works

- **Point:** I am giving away a sofa, free if you collect it.
  **Draft:** 🛋️ Giving away my sofa for 100% free
  
  Only rule is you have to come pick it up yourself and carry it out
  
  DM me if you want it!

- **Point:** The new menu launches next week.
  **Draft:** 🍽️ New menu goes live next week!
  
  Scrapped half the old stuff and added food I actually wanted to eat myself
  
  Feedback welcome!

- **Point:** Someone keeps taking my stapler.
  **Draft:** Someone stole my stapler again so I duct taped an AirTag to it
  
  I know, it looks terrible!

- **Point:** I deleted the social media apps from my phone for a month.
  **Draft:** 📱 Deleted all social media apps from my phone for a month
  
  First week my brain kept reaching for my pocket like an idiot haha
  
  Focus immediately came back and I actually shipped real stuff instead of doomscrolling
  
  Crazy how addicted I was

- **Point:** My train was late again this morning.
  **Draft:** 🚆 Train late again today
  
  Japan trains are timed to the second and here we can't even get one to arrive in the same hour

- **Point:** I am moving house this weekend and need help carrying boxes.
  **Draft:** 📦 Moving this weekend and need people to help carry boxes
  
  Free pizza and beer, promise it's just heavy lifting and no bullshit!
  
  DM me if you're around

- **Point:** I got a parking ticket outside my own house.
  **Draft:** 🚗 Got a parking ticket outside my own house today
  
  Parked right by my front door and city bureaucracy still finds a way to bill you for existing 😂

- **Point:** Our garden finally has tomatoes.
  **Draft:** 🍅 Finally grew actual tomatoes in the garden
  
  Spent months watering plants and stressing over sunlight just to get what the supermarket sells for almost nothing
  
  Still way more rewarding than staring at screens all day

- **Point:** The plumber did not show up.
  **Draft:** 🔧 Plumber just completely ghosted
  
  Sitting at home waiting all morning for someone who just doesn't show up
  
  Ultimate monopoly business

- **Point:** I am taking a week off to do nothing.
  **Draft:** 🏖️ Taking a whole week off to do absolutely nothing
  
  No laptop no side projects no coding
  
  My brain is completely fried and needs a hard reboot I think

- **Point:** I am proud of the team for shipping the update.
  **Draft:** Super proud of the team today!
  
  We just pushed the new update live and it actually works so well
  
  Try it out and tell us what breaks!

- **Point:** I found a parking spot right outside the door for once.
  **Draft:** 🚗 Found a spot literally directly in front of the door today
  
  Felt like winning the lottery honestly, I never get this lucky
