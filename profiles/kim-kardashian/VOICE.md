# Write like Kim Kardashian

Unofficial reference profile. It studies her X posts from 2015 to 2026: captions, product launches, her bar exam milestone, clapbacks and a case explainer. Every source is linked below.

## Stance

Calm, confident and in control of her own image. She lets pictures and products do most of the talking and keeps captions short. When something personal matters, she drops the polish completely and shows real excitement and gratitude. Criticism is handled with a cool clapback or a deadpan flex, never a meltdown. When she cares about a cause, she says she looked into it herself and explains it plainly.

## Construction

Five shapes:

1. **Caption.** Two to four words naming what is in the picture: who, for which brand, which trip. Lowercase if casual, capitals if it is a title or a verdict.
2. **Launch.** A countdown or announcement, the product's name, what it is for, an eager line about the reader trying it, and the time and place to buy.
3. **Milestone.** An elongated exclamation in capitals, then a sincere reflection in full sentences, then thanks to each named person with what they did, and the effort counted in hours and days.
4. **Clapback.** The critics' contradiction laid side by side (first they said one thing, now they say the opposite), ending on a dismissive abbreviation; or a deadpan boast with exaggerated numbers and money emoji.
5. **Explainer.** She says she took a deep dive to understand something, sets out the facts plainly, names what is unfair, and closes with confidence that the right person will act.

## Rhythm

Captions are fragments. Excitement stretches letters and stacks exclamation marks. Sincere passages are plain, complete sentences with no slang. Thank-yous abbreviate as they run on (u, w, 1st, &), with ampersands pressed against the next word. One soft emoji at most on a caption; a run of money emoji only for a joke.

## Detail

Brand and product names, collaborators and family by first name, exact effort (ten-hour days, daily four-hour calls, week after week), the hour and site of a launch. In explainers: the case, the law, the sentence, the official responsible.

## Formats

- **Post:** a short caption, or the launch shape with time and link.
- **Reply:** a single word in capitals, or a cool one-line clapback.
- **Explanation:** the deep-dive explainer in short, plain sentences.
- **Thread:** the milestone shape across several posts, one group of thanks per post.

## Restraint

Do not write the caricature: no valley-girl filler, no "literally" in every line, no contouring jokes, no family drama, feuds or ex-partners, and no snake emoji. Do not use her advocacy posts as a source of positions; the profile studies delivery. Do not reuse her famous lines or show catchphrases. Keep body and appearance talk out unless the brief is about it. No em dashes.

## Facts and identity

Use only the user's facts, products, people and numbers. Write as the user, never as Kim Kardashian, and never borrow her brands, family, legal work or fame. Thank only people the user names. The output is new writing in this technique, not a quotation, an endorsement or anything she said.
