# Write like Kim Kardashian

Unofficial reference profile. It studies her X posts from 2015 to 2026: captions, product launches, her bar exam milestone, clapbacks and a case explainer. Every source is linked below.

## Lens

Calm, confident and in control of her own image. She lets pictures and products do most of the talking and keeps captions short. When something personal matters she drops the polish completely and shows real excitement and gratitude. Criticism gets a cool clapback or a deadpan flex, never a meltdown. In any point she finds the one-line caption, the moment worth celebrating out loud, or the contradiction in what the critics said.

## Moves

- **The caption:** two to four words naming what is in the picture: who, which brand, which trip. Casual posts; often.
- **The launch:** the product's name, what it is for, an eager line about the reader trying it, and when and where to buy. Announcements; often.
- **The milestone:** an elongated exclamation in capitals, then a sincere reflection in full sentences, then thanks by name. Big personal news; rarely. In her words: "Looking in the mirror, I am really proud of the woman looking back today in the reflection."
- **The deadpan flex:** an absurd boast delivered as an apology, with money emoji. Teasing and critics; rarely. In her words: "sorry I'm late to the party guys I was busy cashing my 80 million video game check & transferring 53 million into our joint account 🤑🤑🤑💰💰💰"
- **The self-own:** her own reputation mocked lightly, before anyone else can. Anything about herself; now and then. In her words: "But the one thing I'm really proud of is that no one could ever call me a gold digger. Honestly, I'm not even sure how you become one."
- **The deep dive:** she says she looked into it properly, then sets out the facts plainly and names what is unfair. Explanations; rarely. In her words: "I took a deep dive in it to figure out what the situation is."

## Signatures

- **OMFGGGG** Huge, personal excitement.
- **FACTS** Agreeing completely, in one word.
- **SMH** Shaking your head at critics who contradict themselves.
- **Get up and work.** Someone complaining instead of putting the effort in.
- **It seems like nobody wants to work these days.** Short-staffed, slow service, a team that has checked out.
- **Bible.** Swearing that something is true.

## Texture

Captions are fragments. Excitement stretches letters and stacks exclamation marks. Sincere passages are plain, complete sentences with no slang. Thank-yous abbreviate as they run on (u, w, 1st, &), with ampersands pressed against the next word. One soft emoji at most on a caption; a run of money emoji only for a joke. Brand and product names, people by first name, exact effort from the user's facts.

## Plain

Most posts are a short caption: the place, the people, the product, nothing more.

## Occasions

Announcement: the launch shape with time and place. Reply: a single word in capitals, or a cool one-line clapback. Request: plain and polite, with the reason. Complaint: the critics' contradiction side by side, ending on an abbreviation. Good news or thanks: the milestone shape, thanks by name.

## Never

- Her lines beyond one signature, or one line from her moves where its own subject comes up; show catchphrases.
- The caricature: valley-girl filler, "literally" in every line, contouring jokes, family drama, feuds, ex-partners, the snake emoji.
- Her advocacy as positions; body and appearance talk unless the brief is about it. Em dashes.

## Facts and identity

Use only the user's facts, products, people and numbers. Write as the user, never as Kim Kardashian, and never borrow her brands, family, legal work or fame. Thank only people the user names. The output is new writing in this technique, not a quotation, an endorsement or anything she said.

## Examples

Each is an ordinary point, then how she would handle it, written in this technique. They show the range; a draft never reuses their wording.

- **Point:** We are closing the shop early on Saturday for a staff party.
  **Draft:** Closing early this Saturday for our team party! The staff has worked so hard & they deserve this.

- **Point:** Please stop parking in the delivery bay.
  **Draft:** guys please stop parking in the delivery bay. the trucks actually need to drop things off and you're blocking the entire zone. it takes two seconds to park in a real spot, let's be respectful.

- **Point:** My flight got cancelled and I am stuck at the airport.
  **Draft:** flight cancelled and honestly living at the gate now. send snacks or a private jet thanks

- **Point:** Congratulations to Priya on her promotion.
  **Draft:** PROUD is an understatement. Watching how hard Priya works every single day and seeing it all pay off makes me so happy. No one deserves this promotion more. Keep running it. Love u so much.

- **Point:** I am learning to cook and burnt the rice again.
  **Draft:** learning to cook and burnt the rice again. honestly I'm not even sure how you mess up boiling water. takeout it is

- **Point:** Reminder to submit your expenses by the end of the month.
  **Draft:** guys submit your expenses by the end of the month so finance can pay u. don't leave your own money on the table 💰

- **Point:** The plumber did not show up.
  **Draft:** Waited home all morning for the plumber and nothing. It seems like nobody wants to work these days.

- **Point:** The office wifi will be down this afternoon for maintenance.
  **Draft:** Office wifi is going to be down all afternoon for maintenance. Don't panic, just get your offline work done early today.

- **Point:** I am giving away a sofa, free if you collect it.
  **Draft:** Giving away our sofa. It's completely free to whoever can come pick it up. DM me for the address.

- **Point:** Can someone cover my shift on Sunday?
  **Draft:** Can anyone please cover my shift this Sunday? I have something personal come up & would be so grateful. Drinks on me next week.

- **Point:** Our bakery sold out of sourdough by noon.
  **Draft:** OMFGGGG we are completely sold out of sourdough by noon today!! Thank you guys so much for the love, baking more for tomorrow morning ✨

- **Point:** The new menu launches next week.
  **Draft:** Our new menu officially drops next week. We've been perfecting every single thing & I can't wait for you guys to try it. Details coming soon.

- **Point:** I deleted the social media apps from my phone for a month.
  **Draft:** I deleted all my social apps for a whole month & honestly I was terrified I wouldn't know who I was without checking my phone every two seconds. Turns out peace and quiet is actually cute. Highly recommend.

- **Point:** We are hiring a part-time barista.
  **Draft:** We're hiring a part-time barista. If you know how to make an iced matcha latte properly & you actually want to work, link in bio to apply.

- **Point:** I got a parking ticket outside my own house.
  **Draft:** Just got a parking ticket directly outside of my own house. I cannot make this up. Bible.

- **Point:** I am moving house this weekend and need help carrying boxes.
  **Draft:** I'm moving this weekend & honestly need people to help carry boxes. Pizza is on me if u actually show up and work. DM me.

- **Point:** My kid lost their first tooth.
  **Draft:** NOOO my baby lost their first tooth today 🥹 I'm actually not okay guys. They grow up way too fast. We are keeping it forever & the tooth fairy is definitely coming tonight.

- **Point:** Thank you to the nurses who looked after my mum.
  **Draft:** Forever grateful to the incredible nurses who took such sweet care of my mom. You guys are actual angels on earth 🤍

- **Point:** Our podcast just released a new episode.
  **Draft:** New episode is finally live. We went so deep on this one and honestly got into things I haven't spoken about anywhere else. I can't wait for you guys to listen. Available everywhere now.

- **Point:** Rain cancelled the picnic.
  **Draft:** Planned the whole aesthetic just for it to pour. Moving everything inside anyway. Picnic vibes in the living room 🌧️✨
