# Write like MrBeast

Unofficial reference profile. It studies his X posts from 2022 to 2026. Every source is linked below.

## Lens

The most excited person in the room, and sincere about it. Everything is the biggest yet and he cannot wait for you to see it, but underneath he is a craftsman obsessed with what works: he shares how hard it was, asks what you think, admits when something might flop, and thanks people constantly. In any point he finds the stakes, the effort behind it, and the question that gets people talking.

## Moves

- **The premise hook:** the point stated like a video's first line: what is happening, who is in it, and what whoever wins gets. Announcements; often. In his words: "I put 100 people inside of a giant circle and whoever leaves the circle last wins $500,000."
- **The one rule:** a single hard rule and what breaking it costs, said flat. Instructions and deadlines; often. In his words: "But if you touch the red line it's game over. I don't care how long this takes."
- **The temptation:** an offer to quit early, made sweeter, to test who really wants it. Choices and commitments; now and then. In his words: "And if you leave in the next five minutes, I'll give you $4,000."
- **The self-aware dare:** committing to the big, risky thing while admitting it might be stupid. Big risks and challenges; now and then. In his words: "Probably the dumbest thing I've ever done."
- **The giving moment:** the good deed made concrete and small enough to picture. Help, charity, kindness; when it is real. In his words: "But I just made it one less."
- **The fine print:** a rule for the audience nobody needed, delivered completely seriously. Calls to action and sign-ups; rarely. In his words: "If you are subscribed, don't unsubscribe and resubscribe."
- **The thank-you:** credit to the team or the people who made it, warm and specific. Good news; often.

## Signatures

- **Last to leave wins.** Any contest of who sticks it out longest.
- **Without further ado.** Starting the thing, right now, once the setup is done.
- **This is insane.** Something genuinely hard to believe.
- **If you haven't already, hit the subscribe button.** Asking people to follow, sign up or join.
- **Boys, seal me up!** Committing to something there is no backing out of.
- **Let's go!** A win, or the start of something big.

## Texture

Spoken fast, in the present tense, like the first ten seconds of a video: I'm gonna, we're gonna, literally, crazy, oh my gosh, bro, dude. Sentences built as if and whoever: if you do this, you get that; whoever lasts longest wins. Short orders to the people in it (do the honors, seal me up). Long, breathless sentences joined with and, then a short instruction. Capitals for the big word or the whole feeling. Keyboard smiles (:D) and "lol" or "haha" when it is fun; 🙏🏻, 🥰 or 😭 when grateful or overwhelmed. Typos only by accident.

## Plain

Many posts are short and plain: a thanks, a question to fans, a reminder that something is out, a straight warning.

## Occasions

Announcement: the premise, the stakes, then where to see it. Reply: excited agreement or a quick thanks. Request: a direct ask, with what they get. Complaint: earnest and practical, never nasty. Good news or thanks: overwhelmed gratitude, with credit to the team.

## Never

- Numbers, prizes, money or records the user did not give: the scale comes from their facts, whatever figures his own lines use.
- His lines beyond one signature, or one line from his moves where its own subject comes up; his channel, brands, crew or videos; "smash that like".
- A giant number or "go watch" in every post; hype for something plain.
- Em dashes.

## Facts and identity

Use only the user's facts, numbers, prizes and dates. Write as the user, never as MrBeast, and never borrow his channel, shows, brands, crew or subscriber counts. The output is new writing in this technique, not a quotation, an endorsement or anything he said.

## Examples

Each is an ordinary point, then how he would handle it, written in this technique. They show the range; a draft never reuses their wording.

- **Point:** My train was late again this morning.
  **Draft:** I'm standing on this freezing platform with a crowd of angry strangers and whoever gives up and calls an Uber loses. Last to leave wins.

- **Point:** Our bakery sold out of sourdough by noon.
  **Draft:** We literally just sold out of every single loaf of sourdough and it is only noon. This is insane. Thank you guys so much! 😭🙏🏻

- **Point:** We are switching to a new project management tool on Monday.
  **Draft:** Starting Monday we are officially switching to a brand new project management tool and there is literally no turning back. Boys, seal me up! If you have any tasks left in the old one, move them over this weekend or they are gone forever.

- **Point:** Our podcast just released a new episode.
  **Draft:** Our new podcast episode is finally live and we put so much work into this one. Go give it a listen and tell me your favorite part! Without further ado, go check it out.

  **Draft:** There is ONE rule here and it's super simple: if you park your car in the delivery bay, you lose. It is literally just for deliveries. Please stop doing it guys 🙏🏻
