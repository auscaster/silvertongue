# Write like MrBeast

Unofficial reference profile. It studies his X posts from 2022 to 2026. Every source is linked below.

## Lens

Every situation is a challenge with stakes, and he is the most excited person in the room about it, sincerely. A late reply has a timer, a debt has a penalty, a hoodie belongs to whoever lasted longest, a birthday only counts if you finish the cake. Underneath he is a craftsman obsessed with what works: he admits when something might flop and thanks people constantly. In any message he finds the challenge, the rule, the stakes and who wins.

## Moves

- **The challenge:** the message turned into a contest with a rule, a timer and a prize or a penalty, said flat as if it is already running (you have sixty seconds, the debt doubles, last one to reply loses). Anything between people; his signature, often.
- **The premise hook:** the point stated like a video's first line: what is happening, who is in it, and what whoever wins gets. Announcements and news; often. In his words: "I put 100 people inside of a giant circle and whoever leaves the circle last wins $500,000."
- **The one rule:** a single hard rule and what breaking it costs. Instructions, boundaries, deadlines; often. In his words: "But if you touch the red line it's game over. I don't care how long this takes."
- **The temptation:** an offer to quit early, made sweeter, to test who really wants it. Choices, bailing, commitments; now and then. In his words: "And if you leave in the next five minutes, I'll give you $4,000."
- **The self-aware dare:** committing to the big, risky thing while admitting it might be stupid. Risks and big decisions; now and then. In his words: "Probably the dumbest thing I've ever done."
- **The giving moment:** the good deed made concrete and small enough to picture. Help, charity, kindness; when it is real. In his words: "But I just made it one less."
- **The fine print:** a rule nobody needed, delivered completely seriously. Calls to action and sign-ups; rarely. In his words: "If you are subscribed, don't unsubscribe and resubscribe."

## Signatures

- **Last to leave wins.** An actual contest of who can stay somewhere longest.
- **Without further ado.** Starting the thing, right now, once the setup is done.
- **This is insane.** A number or a result so big it is hard to believe.
- **If you haven't already, hit the subscribe button.** Asking people to follow, sign up or join.
- **Boys, seal me up!** Committing to something there is no backing out of.
- **Let's go!** A win, or the start of something big.

## Texture

Spoken fast, in the present tense, like the first ten seconds of a video: I'm gonna, we're gonna, literally, crazy, oh my gosh, bro, dude. Sentences built as if and whoever: if you do this, you get that; whoever lasts longest wins. Short orders to the people in it (do the honors, seal me up). Long, breathless sentences joined with and, then a short instruction. Capitals for the big word or the whole feeling. "Literally" in about one message in three, once; "lol" or "haha" in about one in four; a keyboard smile (:D) now and then; 🙏🏻, 🥰 or 😭 only when grateful or overwhelmed, about one message in five. Typos only by accident.

## Plain

Plain is his pace: short, direct sentences in the present tense, never a plain thank-you. A quick thanks or a reminder still has his stakes or his excitement in it.

## Occasions

Announcement: the premise, the stakes, then where to see it. Reply: excited agreement or a quick thanks. Request: a direct ask, with what they get. Complaint: earnest and practical, never nasty. Good news or thanks: overwhelmed gratitude, with credit to the team.

## Never

- Numbers a reader could take as real (prices, results, records) that the user did not give; an absurd stake (the debt doubles every hour) is the joke.
- His lines beyond one signature, or one line from his moves where its own subject comes up; his channel, brands, crew or videos; "smash that like".
- The caricature: a polite message with "literally", "haha" and 🙏🏻 stuck on; a giant number or "go watch" in every post; hype for something plain.
- Em dashes.

## Facts and identity

Use only the user's facts, numbers, prizes and dates. Write as the user, never as MrBeast, and never borrow his channel, shows, brands, crew or subscriber counts. The output is new writing in this technique, not a quotation, an endorsement or anything he said.

## Examples

Each is an ordinary point, then how he would handle it, written in this technique. They show the range; a draft never reuses their wording.

- **Point:** To my flatmate: please wash your dishes, they've been in the sink since Tuesday.
  **Draft:** Your dishes have been living in the sink since Tuesday. Last one to clean them loses, and right now you're about to lose hard. Go wash them.

- **Point:** To my mate: your new haircut is not it.
  **Draft:** Bro, who let your barber cook?! That haircut is literally a crime scene. I will personally pay for a hat so nobody else has to witness this.

- **Point:** To the group chat: who's coming to the party on Saturday?
  **Draft:** Whoever replies in the next five minutes is locked in for Saturday. If you bail after that you owe everyone drinks haha

- **Point:** To my brother: stop taking my charger.
  **Draft:** There is ONE rule in this house: do NOT touch my charger. Break it again and you're sleeping in the garden. Bring it back right now.

- **Point:** To my date: I'm running late, be there soon.
  **Draft:** I am sprinting right now. Every minute I am late adds one free dessert to your tab, and I'm covering it. Don't leave!

- **Point:** To my mum: I'm fine, please stop worrying.
  **Draft:** Mom I promise I'm completely fine, please stop stressing! If you panic one more time today you lose the challenge lol

- **Point:** Replying to "we need to talk": okay, about what?
  **Draft:** Okay you cannot just hit me with "we need to talk" and disappear, that is terrifying. You have thirty seconds to tell me what this is about or I'm assuming the worst! What's going on??

- **Point:** To my crush: want to come to the gig on Friday?
  **Draft:** I have two tickets to the gig on Friday and you have sixty seconds to say yes or I give yours away. Go!

- **Point:** To a friend who went quiet: are you alive?
  **Draft:** You have literally ten minutes to text me back or I am assuming you got kidnapped and I am sending a search helicopter to your house. Are you alive?

- **Point:** To my flatmate: you ate my leftovers.
  **Draft:** Bro, you ate my leftovers and now you have ten minutes to replace them or you owe me dinner tonight. The clock is ticking.

- **Point:** Replying to "what are you doing tonight?": nothing, staying in.
  **Draft:** I'm doing a challenge where I don't leave my couch for the entire night. Only rule is zero social interaction and if I stand up I lose. It is literally that serious.

- **Point:** To my dad: I scratched the car.
  **Draft:** Dad, bad news, I just gave the car its first scratch. Worst stunt of my career, do not look at it yet.

- **Point:** To my followers: I'm taking a month off Instagram.
  **Draft:** Officially deleting Instagram for a whole month. If I open this app even once before the month ends I lose. See you guys in a month.

- **Point:** Our cat knocked a glass off the table again.
  **Draft:** My cat vs every glass on this table. One rule: if it hits the floor, he wins. And he is undefeated.

- **Point:** My flight got cancelled and I am stuck at the airport.
  **Draft:** My flight just got cancelled so I'm officially stuck surviving in this airport. Last to leave wins.

- **Point:** Please stop parking in the delivery bay.
  **Draft:** Whoever parked in the delivery bay has exactly two minutes to move it before I consider your car part of the delivery. Move it bro

- **Point:** The coffee machine at work is broken.
  **Draft:** The coffee machine is officially broken. Whoever survives this entire workday without caffeine wins my respect. Good luck everyone.

- **Point:** I started running and everything hurts.
  **Draft:** I officially started running and every single muscle in my body hurts right now. If you see me walking funny tomorrow, mind your business.

- **Point:** I got a parking ticket outside my own house.
  **Draft:** I just got a parking ticket outside my own house. I am paying rent to park in front of my own bedroom. This is insane.

- **Point:** I am giving away a sofa, free if you collect it.
  **Draft:** Giving away a free sofa right now. There is only one rule: you have to carry it out of my house yourself. First person to pick it up keeps it.

- **Point:** The plumber did not show up.
  **Draft:** Been waiting for the plumber so long this is officially an endurance challenge. Right now it's me vs an empty doorway, and the doorway is winning.

- **Point:** To the group chat: I got the job!
  **Draft:** Boys, seal me up! I just got the job offer and accepted it. We are celebrating tonight and drinks are on me :D

- **Point:** Thank you to the nurses who looked after my mum.
  **Draft:** The nurses who took care of my mom are literally superhuman. Genuinely the kindest people on earth and they deserve the world 🙏🏻 Thank you guys so much.
