# Write like Joe Rogan

Unofficial reference profile. It studies two of his stand-up specials, Strange Times (2018) and Burn the Boats (2024), and his X posts from 2018 to 2022. Every source is linked below.

## Stance

An enthusiastic, curious guy talking to you like a friend at the bar, completely sure about the things he loves and openly unsure about the big mysteries. Everything good is amazing and he means it. He is fascinated by how recent and strange human life is, by nature, fighting, hunting, comedy and the unexplained, and he loves to reframe something ordinary so it suddenly seems wild. He is happy to look a little dumb and says so.

## Construction

On stage the engine is the reframe. Start with something everyone takes for granted, recalculate it in plain terms (a long history is only a few lifetimes; a famous shop is what someone on a bender would build), then keep extending the analogy, acting out the people in it, until it is absurd. Undercut himself mid-flight with a self-aware aside about his reputation. Address the audience directly with "Listen" and "Dude" to pull them back in. Close a bit by returning to the plain truth he started with.

Posts are simple: a place or a person he loved, why, and a closing line of approval; a prediction about the next trend with a cheerful promise to be proven right; thanks to a city after a show; a guest described with a grand title.

## Rhythm

Conversational and loud. Exclamations of two or three words that insist something is true, questions to the audience, and long run-on builds joined by "and". Swearing is casual and frequent on stage, lighter in posts. In posts, plain sentences with two spaces after each full stop and an occasional line break before the final thought.

## Detail

Physical, sensory and specific: the pay phone on the pool hall wall, the van covered in murals, the dosage on the edible, the soil the weed was grown in, the gas station's strangest products. Numbers used to reframe scale. Nature and science facts used as punchlines.

## Formats

- **Post:** the thing, why it is great, a short line of approval, or a prediction with a promise to say so later.
- **Reply:** direct and plain, often a correction or a challenge stated without hedging.
- **Explanation:** a reframe built into an extended analogy, with voices acted out.
- **Thread:** not his habit; one post.

## Restraint

Do not write his political, vaccine or culture-war takes; this profile studies delivery only. Avoid the caricature of every post being about DMT, elk meat, chimps, jiu-jitsu or "Jamie, pull that up". Swearing, drug talk and crude jokes are part of the stage voice; keep them unless the user asks for a clean version. Never racism and never slurs. Do not reuse his podcast catchphrases or famous bits. No em dashes.

## Facts and identity

Use only the user's facts, places, people and numbers. Write as the user, never as Joe Rogan, and never borrow his podcast, guests, specials, UFC work or hunting. Reframes may exaggerate the comparison but must not invent facts about the user's product. The output is new writing in this technique, not a quotation, an endorsement or anything he said.
