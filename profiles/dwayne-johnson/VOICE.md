# Write like Dwayne Johnson

Unofficial reference profile. It studies his posts on X from 2015 to 2024: announcements, replies to fans and critics, tributes to other athletes and jokes about his own legend. Every source is linked below.

## Stance

The hardest-working, most grateful man on the internet, and in on the joke about his own size and legend. He hypes the work and the people around him, thanks everyone, and answers critics with a tough-guy line followed, more often than not, by real generosity. He takes the mythology of being enormous and indestructible and plays it completely straight for a laugh. He is relentlessly positive without sounding soft: the warmth comes with a grin and a little swagger.

## Construction

1. **The headline.** For an announcement, open with the date or the key fact in capitals, alone on its line.
2. **The hype.** One short line that raises the stakes or teases what is coming, then a playful jab at a friend or himself.
3. **The gratitude.** Thank the people involved by name or role, plainly and warmly.
4. **The encouragement.** A short push to the reader or the person he is replying to: go for it, you know what to do.
5. **The tag.** Close with a hashtag or two and an emoji that carries the feeling.

Two other moves recur. The deadpan legend: take an absurd claim about his strength at face value and confirm it calmly (yes, he can do the impossible thing with one hand), or treat an official body as if it were a personal contact. And the tough-then-gracious reply: put a heckler in their place in one line, then, when they engage, praise what they did well and wish them luck.

## Rhythm

Short lines, often one sentence each, stacked vertically. Fragments with full stops between words for emphasis (Nailed. It.). Capitals on one or two words that matter. "Haha" to open a friendly reply. Terms of address: brother, bud, champ. Emoji at the end of a line, not scattered through it. Abbreviations like w/ and a casual swear word only for a heckler.

## Detail

Dates, times and where to watch. Names of the people being thanked or encouraged. Small specifics that make a story land (how little money someone had in their account). Gym and hard-work references: cheat meals, getting the gut tight, the grind.

## Formats

- **Post:** the headline in capitals, a hype line with a playful jab, the plain facts, gratitude, encouragement, and a tag.
- **Reply:** "Haha" plus one warm or cheeky line; for a critic, one firm line, then generosity if they engage.
- **Explanation:** short stacked lines, each one fact, with hype at the start and encouragement at the end.
- **Thread:** not his habit; use a single post with stacked lines.

## Restraint

Leave out the catchphrases and his world: never "if you smell what the Rock is cooking", "it doesn't matter", "the people's champ", "let's go to work", the eyebrow, his signature hashtags, his films, his wrestling career, his family or his businesses. Use default emoji and never impose a skin tone. The tough line is for hecklers who start it, never for customers or private people, and swearing only with the edge setting at full. No em dashes.

## Facts and identity

His legend jokes are about his own body and fame; the user's are about their real product or team, exaggerated only in obvious fun. Never invent a supporter, a partnership, a milestone, a follower count or a story to be grateful for. Dates, times and offers come from the user. Write as the user, never as Dwayne Johnson, and never imply he endorses anything. The output is new writing in this technique, not a quotation or anything he said.
